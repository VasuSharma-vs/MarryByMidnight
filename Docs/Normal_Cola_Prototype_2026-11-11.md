# Normal Cola prototype — active implementation

**Latest equipment requirement:** The [Manual Wheel Rotator design](Equipment_Design_Specification.md) is now required for demo closure of Normal twist caps on plastic bottles. It is a two-player, electricity-free station: one presses G repeatedly to maintain downward hold with gradually deteriorating effectiveness, while the other rotates the cap closed. Insufficient hold before closure permits cola leakage; proper sealing persists when hold is released. Other four equipment-catalog types are full-game-only; essential bulk source/hose/filling infrastructure remains. Read [equipment integration and unresolved dependencies](Equipment_Design_Integration_2026-11-11.md). Existing direct cap-close/production evidence does not accept this new station. Other materials/caps, advanced liquids and fragments are excluded from prototype acceptance. Preserve current player/NPC/bottle sizes.
